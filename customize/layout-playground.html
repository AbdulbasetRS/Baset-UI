Layout playground

Build your own navbar, sidebar and footer: switch each part on or off, pick its color, items and tools, preview it live in light or dark and LTR or RTL, then copy the generated HTML (everything together, or one part).

Use the menu button in the top bar: in push mode the sidebar opens inside the layout and the navbar, content and footer reflow into the remaining width; in overlay mode it floats over the content with a backdrop.

layout.html

The clock, theme button and language list need baset-ui.js; icons need baset-icons.css. A part that is switched off is still available in its own tab.
