# Third-party material

## Phosphor Icons

- Used for: the icon artwork in `assets/icons/` and, through the generator, in `assets/css/extensions/baset-ui-icons.css`.
- Source: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core (npm package `@phosphor-icons/core`, version 2.1.1), https://phosphoricons.com
- Subset: 51 icons in six weights (Phosphor "fill" is named `solid` here). The SVG files keep the original artwork; they were copied and renamed to `assets/icons/{weight}/{name}.svg`. The names, categories and tags in `assets/icons/icons.json` come from the Phosphor catalog metadata.
- Phosphor is only the source of the files. It is not a dependency of Baset-UI and nothing is loaded from it at run time.
